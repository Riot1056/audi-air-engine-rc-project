# Audi-Inspired Air Engine and RC Car

A student engineering project inspired by the Audi Sport quattro S1. The long-term goal is to design, build, and test a small compressed-air-powered RC car. The current milestone is a single-cylinder, hand-turned crank and flywheel study model.

## Current status

- A visual single-cylinder concept is available in Tinkercad and as STL/OpenSCAD files.
- The Tinkercad import is one mesh, not separate moving parts.
- The mechanism has not yet been tested as a working assembly.
- Vehicle targets are stretch goals: under 10 lb, at least 10 mph, and 5–7 minutes full-power-equivalent runtime.

## Files

- `index.html` — project showcase and links to the model, lesson, and project brief.
- `Audi_Air_Engine_20min_Voiced_Lesson.html` — interactive narrated lesson.
- `Single_Cylinder_Hand_Turned_Tinkercad.stl` — single-cylinder concept mesh.
- `Single_Cylinder_Hand_Turned_Concept.scad` — parametric OpenSCAD source.
- `Audi_Inspired_Compressed_Air_Engine_Explainer.gif` — animated visual explainer.

## Safety

The printed concept is not a pressure-rated engine, cylinder, or tank. Do not connect it to compressed air. Never 3D print or modify a pressure vessel. Any future pneumatic testing requires commercially rated hardware and qualified teacher/adult review.
